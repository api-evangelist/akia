# Akia

> Akia is an agentic AI product for hospitality that autonomously manages the guest lifecycle — messaging, reservations, upsells, surveys — from booking through checkout. Trusted by 2,000+ operators.

Akia is not a platform with AI bolted on. It is a heartbeat-driven agent that works proactively on behalf of hotel staff, takes action in the PMS and adjacent systems, and escalates when it needs a human.
